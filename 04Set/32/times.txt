Conclusions:
    Precompiled headers offer a large increase in speed with the trade off of significant sizes.
    A single combined SPCH offers even faster compilation speeds with a smaller amount of memory being used because the compiler only parses one large set of headers instead of re-parsing overlapping header content for every class.
    Ccache drastically reduced compilation time as it recognised there where no changes in files since the last compilation and stored them in cache.
    Multithreading also offers a decrease in compilation time but it is not as significant as seen with precompiled headers or a single precompiled header.
